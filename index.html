About Madarsa

Sabeel Us Salaam Online

Learn Quran. Learn Islam. Transform Your Life.

Madarsa Sabeel-us-Salam Online provides various online courses. These courses are taught only through live classes (not recorded ones). The Madarsa has a dedicated team of certified and experienced teachers available for learners at all times through WhatsApp, where they may post queries about anything they have doubts about.

Madarsa Sabeel Us Salam Online, initiated under Ameen Educational Foundation with an eye to the present-day needs and engagements of people, is a significant and religious missionary institution. As time is gliding by, the Ulama are becoming cognizant and aware of the needs of the time. As people are getting more and more preoccupied with the day, they are also complaining of the dearth of time. The engagements of life have shackled people so extremely that it has become difficult for them to travel for the basic religious education rewarding this life and the life hereafter. Consequently, they are bereft of basic Islamic education, too. For this very purpose, the need of such an institution was being felt very strongly, where every person (male and female) could attain Islamic education sparing themselves enough time and from long and exhaustive journeys, so that their beliefs, prayers, other matters and every moment of life could follow the prophetic precepts and be blessed thereupon.

Aims and Objectives

  • To provide the best platform for Islamic Education, equipped with the latest technology.
  • Online publication and promotion of Islamic education.
  • Inviting and preaching the words of Tawheed to every human being.
  • Create a platform to conduct online education and training in a systematic manner.
  • Management of religious education for men and women (at any stage of life).
  • An online community section for questions related to the religion of Islam where they can be answered at any time by an expert team.
  • Create a permanent online platform for women where every subject can be taught by lady teachers only.

How It Started

A number of Muslim brothers and sisters are busy with their businesses and college studies and desire to learn Deen/Islam with a deep understanding, along with their current engagements. This was the real cause and impetus that led to the establishment of Madarsa Sabeel-us-Salam Online. With this aim in mind, Madarsa Sabeel-us-Salam commits itself to provide a trusted and authentic platform competent enough to teach and lace the learners with the best Islamic Education in the comfort of their homes.

Therefore, the classes have been designed keeping learners of all age groups (above 15 years of age) in mind. All learners need is a computer, laptop, or mobile and a fast internet connection — they are all set to learn Deen.
